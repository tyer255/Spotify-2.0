Spotiz respects intellectual-property rights and complies with applicable copyright laws, including the notice-and-takedown principles of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and international copyright standards.

### Scope of Takedown Requests
- **Project-Controlled Material:** If you are a copyright owner or authorized agent and believe that content, code, documentation, or a preconfigured repository list distributed directly via the official Spotiz repository, website, or binary distribution infringes your copyright, you may submit a takedown notice to our contact channel below;

In such cases, notices must be directed to the third-party hosting provider or domain administrator.

### Submitting a Notice
To submit a copyright notice concerning material distributed directly by Spotiz, please provide:

1. Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative;
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed;
3. Clear identification of the specific material or integration list distributed by Spotiz that is claimed to be infringing (including exact URLs or file paths);
4. Contact information (name, address, telephone number, and email address);
5. A statement of good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized;
6. A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

**Submit Notices To:**  
- **Contact:** Official In-App Support & Takedown Portal  
- **Repository:** https://github.com/spotiz/spotiz

Upon receiving a valid and complete notice, maintainers will review the request and take appropriate action, such as delisting, removing, or disabling the identified material from official project distributions.
